==============================================================================
Name a greppable symbol, never a line number.

Steps are vertical slices: extract the piece, rewire every caller, delete the
original, all inside one step. Never move in one step and rewire in the next.
Every step leaves a working app, so work can stop at any boundary.

Verification lives in the step whose work it proves. A step whose work is not
observable on its own moves its gesture down to the step where it becomes
observable, physically -- never a "deferred, see step N" pointer.

Temporary test hooks are either harmless if left in permanently, or removed
in the step that stops needing them. Never parked in a cleanup step that may
never be reached.
